fix(server): stop leaking Mongoose messages through a second syntax

The previous claim that nothing else leaked was wrong. That sweep grepped for
"message: error.message" and removed ten sites. That is one spelling, not the
bug: 21 more used "error: error instanceof Error ? error.message" -- the same
disclosure, same response body, different syntax. Two more hid behind helpers:
inventoryController "message(error, fallback)" returned the Mongoose text at all
five of its call sites, and warehouseController passed error.message as the
response message outright.

So 23 additional disclosure sites across AddressController, OrderController,
ReviewController, adminController, inventoryController, productController and
warehouseController.

The fix is deliberately the smallest one that removes the disclosure: drop the
field. No signature change, no control-flow change. Two earlier attempts
converted these catch blocks to next(error) and both had to be reverted after
mangling the handler signatures.

Eight sites remain, every one guarded by instanceof InsufficientStockError |
ReceiveError | TransferError. Those are domain errors whose messages were
written for a user, which is why a blanket rule would have been wrong.

Removing the field left "catch (error)" unused in 18 places, which eslint
caught. Each now logs the error, so the detail stays available to us and out of
the response.
